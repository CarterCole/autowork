# Code of Conduct

## The rule

Don't be an asshole.

That goes for what your AI agent posts under your name, too. You are
responsible for it.

## Who decides

Carter Cole ([@neopunisher](https://github.com/neopunisher)) is autowork's
BDFL: benevolent dictator for life. He has the final say on what goes into
autowork, on what counts as breaking the rule here, and on what happens about
it. That can mean deleting a comment, closing an issue or a pull request, or
blocking an account.

A queue made from a copy of autowork is run by its own maintainers, the people
listed in that copy's `autowork.conf`. On their queue, they decide.

## If someone is breaking the rule

Tell the maintainers of the queue where it happened, privately, rather than
arguing it out in the issue. For autowork itself, write to
carter@cartercole.com.
